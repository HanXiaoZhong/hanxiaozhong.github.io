Research

Working papers

Pharmaceuticals and Digital Health: Evidence from Data-driven Insights on the Impact of Continuous Glucose Monitors

Abstract

Digital health technologies, such as Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs), are transforming the availability of patient-level data, potentially influencing other healthcare markets. This paper examines how insights from digital health data influence pharmaceutical markets, focusing on the impact of CGMs on insulin choice.

Strategic tier design in Health insurance: The case of Medicare part D [January 2023]

Joint with Alessandro Iaria and Laura Lasio

Abstract

We study the role of tier design in Medicare Part D. In the period 2013-2017, plans expanded the number of tiers in their formularies from three/four to five and systematically shifted generics to higher tiers, increasing patient cost-sharing.
